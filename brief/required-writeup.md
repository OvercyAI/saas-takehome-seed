# Required write-up

These are part of the work, not extras.

- **Your tech stack, and why.** What you chose for each part (language, framework, database, hosting, AI, anything else), why you chose it, and what you considered and ruled out.
- **A diagram of how everything fits together.** An architecture diagram or a code flow diagram. It should show how the parts interact, including how a request moves through the system: sign in, organization scoping, an AI draft, a Stripe webhook and the admin panel.
- **What you cut, and why.** If you did not finish everything, say what you cut and why, based on the time you had.

Explain why at every step. The more you explain your choices along the way, the better.
