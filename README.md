# Deskly

Thanks for taking the time to do this. You have **5 to 7 days** from when you received this repo. Send it back whenever you are done within that window.

## What you are building

Deskly is a small helpdesk for businesses. A company signs up, invites its team, and works customer support tickets together. When an agent opens a ticket, Deskly can use AI to draft a reply. Companies pay for Deskly with a plan, and the plan limits how many AI drafts they can use each month. The Deskly team runs the whole platform from an admin panel.

You decide how to build it. Pick any language, framework, database, hosting setup and architecture you like. We care more about how you think and what you decide than about which tools you pick.

## How to work

- **Use AI coding agents.** We expect it. You do not need to hide it.
- **Edit `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` however you like.** They are yours, as long as what you build covers the feature list end to end.
- **Show your work, and explain why at every step.** This matters most. Keep a trail in the repo of the decisions you made and why, what you ruled out, the tests you wrote, and how you checked that it works. Put it wherever makes sense to you.
- **Scope honestly.** Plan on about 15 to 20 hours across the week. Doing fewer things well beats doing everything shallowly. You do not have to finish in 7 days. If you do not, say what you cut and why, based on the time you had.
- **Do not commit secrets.** Use environment variables and tell us what we need to set.

Questions about the brief are welcome. Ask early rather than guess.
